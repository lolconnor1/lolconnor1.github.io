PROJECT P

A tiny RPG about real projects. Explore the town, talk to the trainers guarding GenGO!, Post Guardian, Pulsefex, and Baseball Oracle — then beat them in battle with your trusty Rubber Duck.

WASD / Arrows to move  ·  SPACE to talk & confirm

A PORTFOLIO IN GAME FORM

Explore the town

Talk to PROF. PIXEL at the plaza first. Each building belongs to one of Connor's real projects — read the signs, then talk to the trainer out front.

Battle for knowledge

Every trainer tells their project's true story, then challenges you. Their creatures fight with moves pulled straight from the real tech stack.

Clear all four

Rubber Duck knows DEBUG, REFACTOR, STACK OVERFLOW, and COFFEE BREAK. Beat all four projects and report back to the Professor to finish the tour.
